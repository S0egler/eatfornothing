Privacy Policy

Last updated: 10 October 2026

What this app does

"EFN Poster" is a private tool used only by the operator of the Eat for Nothing accounts to publish their own recipe videos and images to their own TikTok and Instagram accounts. It is not offered to the public and no other people can sign in to it.

Data we process

To upload content, the tool stores the access and refresh tokens that TikTok and Instagram issue for the operator's own accounts. Tokens are kept encrypted or in secret storage and are used only to publish content and to read basic statistics of these accounts.

We do not collect, store or sell any data about other users, viewers or followers.

Sharing

Data is shared only with the platforms we publish to (TikTok, Instagram) as required to upload content. We do not share data with third parties.

Deletion

Access can be revoked at any time in the TikTok or Instagram app settings. Stored tokens are then deleted.

Contact

Questions: send a direct message to @eatfornothing on Instagram.
