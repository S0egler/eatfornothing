Shakshuka

Dinner for ~€1.60? Shakshuka 🍳
Eggs poached in a spicy tomato sauce.

⏱ 25 min  ·  🍽 Serves 2

INGREDIENTS
• 1 tbsp olive oil
• 1 onion
• 1 red bell pepper
• 2 garlic cloves
• 1 can (400 g) chopped tomatoes
• 1 tsp sweet paprika
• 1 tsp ground cumin
• 1 pinch chili flakes (optional)
• Salt & pepper
• 4 eggs
• Parsley & bread to serve

STEPS
1. Dice the onion and bell pepper, finely chop the garlic.
2. Heat the oil in a pan. Fry onion and pepper for 5 min, then add garlic and spices for 1 more minute.
3. Add the tomatoes, season with salt and pepper and simmer uncovered for 10 min until the sauce thickens.
4. Make 4 wells with a spoon and crack an egg into each.
5. Cover and cook on low heat for 5–8 min: whites set, yolks still runny.
6. Sprinkle with parsley and serve straight from the pan with bread.

💡 Leftover tip: feta, spinach or chickpeas go perfectly with it.

Pumpkin Soup

Dinner for ~€1.00? Pumpkin Soup 🍳
Silky Hokkaido soup with coconut milk.

⏱ 35 min  ·  🍽 Serves 4

INGREDIENTS
• 1 (approx. 1 kg) Hokkaido pumpkin
• 1 onion
• 2 garlic cloves
• 1 tbsp oil
• 1 tsp grated ginger (optional)
• 750 ml vegetable stock
• 200 ml coconut milk
• Salt, pepper & nutmeg
• 2 tbsp pumpkin seeds

STEPS
1. Halve the pumpkin, scoop out the seeds and cut it into cubes. The skin can stay on.
2. Chop onion and garlic and fry them in the oil in a large pot for 3 min.
3. Add pumpkin and ginger, fry 2 more min, then pour in the stock.
4. Cover and simmer for 20 min until the pumpkin is soft.
5. Add the coconut milk and blend until smooth.
6. Season with salt, pepper and nutmeg and top with pumpkin seeds.

💡 In season now: pumpkins are cheapest from September to November.

Banana Pancakes

Breakfast for ~€0.75? Banana Pancakes 🍳
Fluffy, naturally sweet, no sugar added.

⏱ 15 min  ·  🍽 Serves 2

INGREDIENTS
• 2 ripe bananas (+1 to serve)
• 3 eggs
• 60 g rolled oats
• 1 pinch cinnamon
• 1 tsp oil for frying

STEPS
1. Mash 2 bananas with a fork until almost smooth.
2. Whisk in the eggs, oats and cinnamon. Let the batter rest for 5 min.
3. Heat a little oil in a non-stick pan over medium heat.
4. Add 2 tbsp of batter per pancake and fry for 2 min per side until golden. Flip gently: they are softer than normal pancakes.
5. Serve with the sliced third banana.

💡 The browner the bananas, the sweeter the pancakes.

Egg Fried Rice

Dinner for ~€1.10? Egg Fried Rice 🍳
The best way to use leftover rice.

⏱ 15 min  ·  🍽 Serves 2

INGREDIENTS
• 300 g cooked rice, cold (100 g dry)
• 3 eggs
• 150 g frozen peas, carrots & corn
• 2 spring onions
• 2 garlic cloves
• 2 tbsp soy sauce
• 1 tbsp oil

STEPS
1. Heat the oil in a large pan or wok over high heat.
2. Fry the garlic and the white parts of the spring onions for 1 min.
3. Add the frozen vegetables and stir-fry for 3 min.
4. Push everything to the side, crack in the eggs and scramble them.
5. Add the cold rice and soy sauce and stir-fry for 3–4 min until hot and slightly crispy.
6. Top with the green spring onion rings.

💡 Use cold rice from the fridge: freshly cooked rice turns mushy.

Potato omelette, warm or cold.

⏱ 40 min  ·  🍽 Serves 4

INGREDIENTS
• 600 g potatoes
• 1 onion
• 6 eggs
• 5 tbsp olive oil
• 1 tsp salt

STEPS
1. Peel the potatoes and slice them thinly. Slice the onion.

5. Slide onto a plate, flip back into the pan and cook for 4–5 more min.
6. Let it cool a little and cut into wedges.
