Eat for Nothing

Connect your TikTok account

Eat for Nothing turns our daily budget recipes into short videos. After you connect your TikTok account, each new recipe video is sent to your TikTok inbox as a draft. Nothing is posted publicly: you review the draft, edit it if you like, and publish it yourself in the TikTok app.

What we ask for

  • user.info.basic: to show which account is connected.
  • video.upload: to send recipe videos to your TikTok inbox as drafts.

You can disconnect at any time in TikTok under Settings and privacy › Security › Manage app permissions.
